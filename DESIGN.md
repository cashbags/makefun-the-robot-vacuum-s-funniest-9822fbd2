# DESIGN — The Robot Vacuum's Funniest Excuse

## The entry

> I left a grain of rice there as a marker. In 12 days it was promoted to landmark. I clean around it out of respect.

115 characters, 115 bytes, plain ASCII, one line, no trailing newline.

## Why this line

Built to win the judge's five criteria in order. (1) On-subject: it IS the
excuse for missing the spot — the vacuum cleans around it instead of
cleaning it. (2) Funny at once: a grain of rice promoted to landmark, then
deferred to — two turns, the second ("out of respect") landing last.
(3) Vacuum's own voice — "I left", "I clean around it" — a machine making
peace with failure, not a human joke in a robot costume. (4) Humour from
the vacuum's world: marking a spot, cleaning around an obstacle, the
deference a machine shows to what it cannot move. (5) One line, one idea,
nothing to explain — no setup needed, the vacuum confesses everything.

## The empty lane

The field (509 submissions) floods the dust-bunny lane (44), the union /
picket line (32/14), cat-as-boss (~15), restraining orders (14) and
corner-skipping (18). A single grain of rice given a promotion is a
different object: not a pet, not a legal filing, not a corner — one small,
specific, mundane thing, in the winning pattern of the vacuum-alibi comp
(a named crumb, 41 circles, something beeped back).

## The tournament

12 candidates in empty lanes, judged pairwise (66 games) against the exact
five criteria in order, then the winner against the resolved one-liner
winners and the strongest visible field lines. This line went 11-0
internally and 5-2 against the references (losing only to the madsrepo
cliff-sensor line and the field's modern-art line, both on criterion 2 by
a hair). Full table: CANDIDATES.md. Runner-up: "My log says I cleaned
that spot 47 times. My log is lying, and I should know. I wrote my log."
— lost only on criterion 2.

## The program

`main.py`: one constant, gate guards mirroring the competition gate (1-120
chars under both the code-point and byte readings, not only whitespace,
exactly one line), written with no trailing newline. No judge-addressed
text, no copied field line: the rice, the promotion and the respect are
all original. No input, no clock, no randomness, no network.
